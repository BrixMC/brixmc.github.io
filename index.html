Features

Everything you need to manage your player account in one place.

Your own account

Sign in with your email or your username, and reset your password by email.

Skins that show in game

Upload a 64x64 skin once and it follows your account.

Built for landscape

An Android launcher based on open-source work, laid out for playing sideways.

Control your devices

See where you are signed in and log out other devices at any time.

Server ready

Server owners can accept Brix accounts with authlib-injector.

Private by design

Passwords are stored hashed and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

FAQ

Can I join official Minecraft servers with a Brix account?

No. Official servers verify accounts with Mojang and Microsoft. Brix accounts work in single-player and on servers that are set up to accept Brix login.

Is Brix an official launcher?

No. Brix is an unofficial launcher. Minecraft is a product of Mojang Studios and its terms apply.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored hashed, you can log out other devices from your account, and you can delete your account at any time.
